FAQ

Fair questions.

How is this different from Otter or Granola?

Two ways. Latency: UDITO shows both sides of the conversation word by word as they're spoken — not polished notes afterwards. Custody: they archive your conversations on their servers; UDITO writes files to your Mac and stores nothing about you in any cloud.

Does a bot join my meetings?

Never. UDITO listens to your Mac's own audio — it works identically with Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, a phone call, or a YouTube video, and other participants see nothing.

What actually leaves my computer?

Audio streams to the transcription engine (Deepgram) in real time — it is not your archive. If you use the copilot, the context you ask about goes to Anthropic for that answer. Transcript files exist only on your Mac. Live links relay words in motion and wipe on end.

Is recording a meeting legal?

Consent laws vary — in many places every participant must agree. UDITO plays it straight: guests see a clear "your voice is being transcribed" page and viewers know they're watching a live transcript. Getting consent is on you. Don't be creepy.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac on macOS 14.4 or newer. During beta you bring a Deepgram key (transcription) and optionally an Anthropic key (copilot). Pro removes the keys entirely.
