FAQ

Fair questions.

How is this different from Otter or Granola?

Two ways. First, latency: UDITO shows both sides of the conversation word by word as they're spoken, not polished notes afterwards. Second, custody: they archive your conversations on their servers; UDITO writes files to your Mac and stores nothing about you in any cloud.

Does a bot join my meetings?

Never. UDITO listens to your Mac's own audio — it works identically with Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, a phone call, or a YouTube video, with nothing visible to other participants.

What leaves my computer?

Audio streams to the transcription engine (Deepgram) in real time and is not retained as your archive; if you use the AI copilot, the transcript context you ask about is sent to Anthropic for that answer. Transcript files live only on your Mac. Live links relay words in motion and wipe when ended.

Is recording a meeting legal?

Consent laws vary — in many places every participant must agree. UDITO gives you the tools (guests see a clear "you're being transcribed" page; share viewers know they're watching a live transcript), but obtaining consent is your responsibility. Don't be creepy.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac on macOS 14.4 or newer. During beta you bring your own Deepgram key (transcription) and optionally an Anthropic key (AI). The Pro plan will remove the keys entirely.
