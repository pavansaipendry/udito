Foundation

Built for the conversation,
not the recording

One native window, from the first word spoken to the file on your disk

UDITO listens to your microphone and the meeting audio at once. Nothing joins the call, no bot appears in the participant list, and nobody installs anything. A built-in copilot answers questions in your voice mid-meeting, remote guests stream in with a six-digit code, and when you press stop the transcript is already a file on your disk. No account, no cloud archive, ever.

Interviews

The question lands in the transcript, and the copilot drafts the answer in your voice, regular or STAR, while they're still finishing the sentence.
