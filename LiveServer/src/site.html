07 — FAQFair questions
How is this different from Otter or Granola?

Latency and custody. UDITO shows both sides of the conversation word by word as they're spoken — not notes afterwards. And they archive your conversations on their servers; UDITO writes files to your Mac and stores nothing about you in any cloud.

Does a bot join my meetings?

Never. UDITO listens to your Mac's own audio — Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, a phone call, a video. Other participants see nothing.

What actually leaves my computer?

Audio streams to the transcription engine (Deepgram) in real time — it is not your archive. If you use the copilot, the context you ask about goes to Anthropic for that answer. Transcript files exist only on your Mac. Live links relay words in motion and wipe on end.

Is recording a meeting legal?

Consent laws vary — in many places every participant must agree. UDITO plays it straight: guests see a clear "your voice is being transcribed" page, and viewers know they're watching a live transcript. Getting consent is on you.

macOS says the app is from an unidentified developer?

The beta isn't Apple-notarized yet (that ships with the paid release). Right-click the app → Open → Open once, and macOS remembers.
